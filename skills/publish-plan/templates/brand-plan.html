Trade-offs and why

Why

  • One project: both teams ship the same app. Separate projects would duplicate every shared flag.
  • Groups in the identity provider, mapping in code: one place to add a person.

Trade-offs

  • Every team sees every folder.
  • Per-folder permissions need the paid plan.
